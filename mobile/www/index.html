Checking the reminder windows...

The website checks certificate expiry dates once a day and emails students a reminder before their certificate runs out. Each certificate gets two reminders: one when it is 30 days from expiring, and another when it is 7 days away. The list below shows the certificates sitting in one of those two windows right now, so these are the students the next daily checks will email. Each reminder goes out only once per certificate: a student who already got the 30 day reminder will not get it again, and will get the 7 day reminder when their certificate reaches that window instead.
